Cookies, or similar techniques, that we use

Sopra Steria uses functional, analytical and tracking cookies. A cookie is a small text file that is stored in the browser of your computer, tablet or smartphone when you first visit this website.

Functional

These cookies ensure that the website works properly and that, for example, your preferred settings are remembered. These cookies are always present.

Analytical

We use google analytics to analyze browsing data to improve our website.

Marketing

In addition, we place cookies that keep track of your surfing behavior so that we can offer customized content and advertisements.

Rejecting cookies

On your first visit to our website, we asked for your permission to place them. You can also reject them and adapt your choice later with the link from the footer. Rejecting cookies means only functional cookies will be used during your visit(s). This setting will be stored in a functional cookie, so you won't be asked twice.

In addition, you can also delete all information that was previously stored via the settings of your browser. For more information (NL):
Veilig internetten en cookies

Third parties

Sopra Steria uses a third party to collect and analyze browsing data from our website for analysis and personal advertisements. If you have your cookies disabled (and you can edit this setting with the link in the footer), your behavior on the site won't be measured.

Special or sensitive personal data

Our website and/or service do not intend to collect data about website visitors who are younger than 16 years old. Unless they have parental or guardian permission. However, we cannot check whether a visitor is older than 16. We therefore advise parents to be involved in the online activities of their children, in order to prevent data about children being collected without parental consent. If you are convinced that we have collected personal information about a minor without that permission, please contact us via To The Root and we will delete this information.

View, modify or delete data

You have the right to view, correct or delete your personal data. In addition, you have the right to withdraw your consent to the data processing or to object to the processing of your personal data by Sopra Steria and you have the right to data portability. This means that you can submit a request to us to send the personal data we have about you in a computer file to you or another organization mentioned by you.

You can send a request for access, correction, deletion, data transfer of your personal data or request for withdrawal of your consent or objection to the processing of your personal data to To The Root.

To ensure that the request for access has been made by you, we ask you to send a copy of your proof of identity with the request. Make your passport photo, MRZ (machine readable zone, the strip with numbers at the bottom of the passport), passport number and Citizen Service Number (BSN) black in this copy. This is to protect your privacy. We will respond to your request as soon as possible, but within four weeks.

Sopra Steria would also like to point out that you have the option to file a complaint with the national supervisory authority, the Dutch Data Protection Authority. You can do that via the following link:
Contact autoriteitpersoonsgegevens

What rights do you have regarding the processing of your personal data?

If you have any questions about what information is collected about you and for what purpose, To The Root and Sopra Steria are always willing to provide you with information. You have the right to inspect and correct or delete data or to restrict the processing of your personal data, as well as the right to object to such processing and the right to data portability. You also have the right to withdraw your consent. If you have any of the above requests, please submit them in writing to support.privacy@soprasteria.com, with info@totheroot.io in the CC. Finally, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority.

Get in touch
